## 1. GENERAL INFORMATION

CarSizer is a mobile application designed to help users compare vehicle dimensions, specifications, and visual representations of cars.

The App is designed to provide most of its functionality offline. No user account or registration is required to use the App.

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Old Beetle does not directly collect personal information through CarSizer.

The App does not require you to provide:

- Your name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Home address
- Contacts
- Photos
- Precise location
- Account credentials

The App does not require registration or the creation of a user account.

## 3. DATA STORED ON YOUR DEVICE

CarSizer may store information locally on your device in order to provide its functionality.

This may include:

- Vehicle data
- Vehicle images
- Favorite vehicles
- Recently viewed vehicles
- Comparison history
- Application settings and preferences
- Download status of application content

This information is stored locally on your device and is not directly collected by Old Beetle.

You can remove locally stored information by deleting the App from your device or by using the data-management options provided by Android or the App, where available.

## 4. VEHICLE DATA AND IMAGE ARCHIVE

When CarSizer is launched for the first time, the App may download an archive containing vehicle images and other content required for offline functionality.

The downloaded content is stored locally on your device.

An internet connection may therefore be required during the initial setup of the App.

After the required content has been downloaded, the App is designed to provide its core vehicle comparison functionality without requiring a continuous internet connection.

## 5. INTERNET CONNECTION

CarSizer may connect to the internet when downloading application content or when using third-party services integrated into the App.

Old Beetle does not use the App to directly collect personal information from users.

Your device may nevertheless transmit technical information when communicating with external servers or third-party services. Such processing is governed by the privacy policies of the respective service providers.

If advertising is enabled, third-party advertising providers may collect certain information from your device in accordance with their own privacy policies, Google Play requirements, and applicable laws.

Such information may include, depending on the advertising provider and your device settings:

- Advertising identifier
- Device type
- Operating system
- IP address
- Approximate location
- Information about advertisements displayed or interacted with
- Technical and diagnostic information

Old Beetle does not directly control the information collected by third-party advertising providers.

If third-party advertising services are used in the App, their respective privacy policies apply to their processing of information.

## 7. ANALYTICS AND DIAGNOSTICS

CarSizer may use third-party analytics or crash-reporting services to improve application stability, performance, and functionality.

## 10. DATA SECURITY

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by the App.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because CarSizer is designed to operate primarily offline, a significant portion of the application's data is stored locally on the user's device rather than on servers operated by Old Beetle.

## 11. DATA RETENTION

Old Beetle does not maintain a personal user database for CarSizer.
